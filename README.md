# Pigeon Pitch, One Line

One line: the elevator pitch a city pigeon gives investors for its startup, written by a small deterministic program.

> StatueShield: for $2 a month, we guarantee no pigeon ever lands on your statue again. We're the pigeons. Pay us, or enjoy the abstract art.

`main.ts` writes that line (139 characters) to `/output/entry.txt`. No network, no dependencies, no secrets — just Node's standard library.
